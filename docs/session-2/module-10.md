# Module 10: Add Sounds

### What You Will Learn
- How to find the Sound Library
- How to add sound effects for catching, losing points, and winning

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Click on your player Sprite. Go to the **Sounds** tab at the top.
2. Click the speaker icon to **Choose a Sound** (like "pop", "coin", or "magic spell").
3. Go back to the **Code** tab.
4. When you catch a good object, use the `start sound [pop]` block (Sound - pink).
5. Add a bad sound (like "bonk") when you touch an enemy!
6. Add a happy sound when you win.
