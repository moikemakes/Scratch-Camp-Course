# Module 1: Explore Scratch

### What You Will Learn
- What the Scratch screen looks like
- What a Sprite is
- How to find coding blocks
- How to change the Backdrop (background)

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Open a new project in Scratch.
2. Look at the left side: This is where all your **Blocks** are.
3. Look at the middle: This is your **Code Area** where you drag blocks to build your script.
4. Look at the top right: This is the **Stage** where your game happens.
5. Look at the bottom right: This is the **Sprite Area**. A Sprite is your game character or object!
