# Module 2: Move Your Player

### What You Will Learn
- How to add controls for your player
- How to make your player move left and right

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. Pick a Sprite to be your player.
2. Go to **Events** (yellow) and drag out `when [right arrow] key pressed`.
3. Go to **Motion** (blue) and drag `change x by 10`. Attach it under the event block.
4. Go to **Events** again and drag out `when [left arrow] key pressed`.
5. Go to **Motion** and drag `change x by -10`. Attach it under the event block.
